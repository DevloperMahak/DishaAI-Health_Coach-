1. About RHEA AI

RHEA AI is an AI-enabled health, wellness and digital assistance platform. Depending on the features made available to a user, the Services may include Medical Consultation with registered medical practitioners, Wellness & Guidance, AI-generated text/image/video/audio/document content, user profiles, communications, subscriptions and payments.

2. Data Fiduciary / Responsible Entity

The entity responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data is: V Provider, operating the RHEA AI platform.

3. Personal Data We May Collect

  • Identity and account information: name, age/date of birth where required, gender where voluntarily provided, profile information and account identifiers.
  • Contact information: mobile number, email address and communication preferences.
  • Authentication information: OTP-related information and login/session details.
  • Health and medical information: symptoms, health history, medical reports, prescriptions, diagnoses, consultation records and other health information when a user chooses to use Medical Consultation or related features.
  • Wellness information: nutrition, fitness, yoga, meditation, sleep, lifestyle, counselling, parenting, senior-wellness and other information voluntarily provided to use relevant services.
  • User content: prompts, text, images, videos, audio, documents and other files uploaded or generated through the Services.
  • Transaction information: subscription/payment status, transaction identifiers and limited payment-related information received from payment service providers. Full card/UPI credentials may be processed directly by the relevant payment provider and not stored by RHEA AI unless specifically stated.
  • Technical and usage information: device information, operating system, app version, IP address, logs, approximate location where enabled, crash reports, analytics and interaction data.
  • Communications and support information: messages, feedback, complaints, support tickets and related correspondence.

4. How We Use Personal Data

  • To create and manage user accounts and authenticate users.
  • To provide, personalize and improve the Services.
  • To facilitate Medical Consultation and securely transmit relevant information to the selected registered medical practitioner.
  • To provide Wellness & Guidance and other requested features.
  • To generate AI outputs requested by the user and operate supporting AI, cloud, analytics and content-processing infrastructure.
  • To process subscriptions, payments, refunds and transaction-related support.
  • To communicate service updates, security alerts, support responses and other necessary notices.
  • To detect fraud, misuse, security incidents and unauthorized activity.
  • To comply with applicable law, lawful requests and regulatory obligations.
  • To create aggregated or de-identified analytics where permitted and reasonably designed so that individuals are not identified.

5. Notice, Consent and User Choices

Where consent is required under applicable law, RHEA AI will seek consent through a clear notice describing the personal data involved and the specified purpose. Where applicable, users may withdraw consent through the mechanisms made available by RHEA AI. Withdrawal will not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal and may affect the availability of a feature that depends on that processing.

6. Medical Consultation and Health Data

Medical Consultation is provided by appropriately registered medical practitioners. RHEA AI may facilitate the technology, scheduling, communication and transmission of information but does not replace the professional judgment of the medical practitioner.

RHEA AI's AI/ML technology is not intended to independently counsel a patient or prescribe medicines. Any medical counselling, diagnosis or prescription that is provided through the telemedicine service is to be delivered by the relevant registered medical practitioner in accordance with applicable requirements.

Health and medical information is treated as sensitive in nature and access is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant service, subject to applicable law and the user's choices.

7. AI Features and User Content

RHEA AI may use third-party AI, cloud, storage, speech, image/video, analytics and other technology providers to process user inputs and generate requested outputs. The exact providers may change as the Services evolve. Where required, appropriate contractual, technical and organizational safeguards will be used.

Users should avoid uploading information that is not necessary for the requested feature. Users remain responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to upload content belonging to another person.
